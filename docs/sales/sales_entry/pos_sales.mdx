# POS Sales

The **POS Sales** screen shows the sales transactions that come from your point-of-sale machines.

---

## When Is the Data Available?

POS sales data appears in Cloud ERP **only after End of Day (EOD) is completed** from:

* **SmartPOS 7**
* **RestPOS 7**

Until EOD is done on the POS machine, its sales will not be visible here.

---

## Steps

### 1. Complete EOD on the POS

* Run **End of Day** from SmartPOS 7 or RestPOS 7.

### 2. Open POS Sales in Cloud ERP

1. Go to **Sales** → **Sales Entry** → **POS Sales**.
2. Filter by date, location or POS machine.
